✦ Privacy & Performance

Your Audio Never Leaves Your Machine

Engineered for creators who demand complete data sovereignty, low latency, and zero installation friction.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike conventional cloud editors that force you to upload your stems to remote servers, Mixota decodes and processes all audio in your computer's local RAM.

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Audio buffers never transmit over the network.
  • Absolute Confidentiality: Ideal for podcasts, unreleased music, voice memos, and proprietary audio.
  • Offline Capability: Operates seamlessly without an internet connection after load.

WebAssembly Engine Specs

Mixota leverages low-level C/C++ audio code compiled directly to WebAssembly for native near-metal performance.

  • Wasm Core: libFLAC, lamejs MP3, and RNNoise AI compiled to native binary bytecode.
  • Web Workers: Non-blocking background threads for heavy rendering and spectrum analysis.
  • Ultra Lightweight: Runtime bundle under 100KB compressed, zero bloated frameworks.
