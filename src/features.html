01 • MULTITRACK DAW

Multi-Track Arrangement

Layer vocal stems, backing tracks, sound effects, and podcast dialogues across unlimited independent audio lanes with non-destructive mixing.

  • Individual lane volume, pan & mute/solo
  • Non-destructive audio clip dragging
  • Direct stem bouncing & stereo mixdown
02 • SURGICAL EDITING

Sample-Accurate Waveform

Zoom smoothly from full-song arrangement down to individual microsecond audio samples with 60fps canvas rendering and zero-crossing detection.

  • Cut, copy, paste, trim & insert silence
  • Interactive logarithmic fade in/out curves
  • Zero-crossing snapping to prevent audio clicks
03 • NEURAL RESTORATION

RNNoise AI Audio Repair

State-of-the-art recurrent neural network trained on thousands of hours of speech eliminates fan noise, air conditioner hum, and room acoustics.

  • Up to -30dB ambient background suppression
  • 50Hz / 60Hz mains electrical hum removal
  • 100% private, client-side WebAssembly inference
04 • SPECTRAL SHAPING

Parametric EQ & Analyzer

Sculpt timbre with precision multi-band peaking and shelving filters alongside a real-time 32-band FFT frequency analyzer.

  • High-pass, low-pass, peaking & notch filters
  • Real-time FFT audio spectrum visualizer
  • Docked tool or pop-out window mode
05 • TEMPO & GROOVE

Beat Detection & Tempo

Analyze rhythmic transients with automated BPM estimation, lock selections to musical beat grids, and tap rhythms in real time.

  • Automated tempo analysis (transient peak energy)
  • Interactive Tap Tempo with average BPM lock
  • Musical snapping for seamless audio loops
06 • CODECS & EXPORT

Universal Audio Codecs

Encode and decode audio formats entirely in the browser using LAME MP3, libFLAC, and 32-bit float WAV engines.

  • MP3 CBR & VBR up to 320kbps

  • Lossless FLAC & native .mixota session files
