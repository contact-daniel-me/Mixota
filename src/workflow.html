Production Architecture

Mixota’s four-stage pipeline is structured around speed, audio fidelity, and intuitive creative exploration.

Open Studio Now View All Features →
1
STAGE 01 • INGESTION & CAPTURE
Zero Server Upload

Import, Record & Inspect

Drop any audio file into your browser or record straight from your microphone using the ultra-low latency AudioWorklet engine. Waveforms decode in parallel in memory without touching a remote server.

Drag-and-Drop Ingest

Accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, WebM, AAC, and previous .mixota project sessions.

Live Microphone Recording

Real-time audio input capture with level metering and hardware-calibrated sample rates.

Instant Waveform Render

High-resolution multiscale peak caching renders full audio tracks in milliseconds.

2
STAGE 02 • RESTORATION & SURGERY
Sample Accuracy

Edit, Repair & Remove Noise

Perform micro-edits with zero-crossing precision. One click activates RNNoise deep neural network speech restoration, wiping out background HVAC rumble, room resonance, and microphone hiss.

Surgical Trimming

Zoom to single audio samples. Cut, crop, copy, paste, and insert silence with ease.

RNNoise AI Cleaning

Eliminate environmental noise with 100% private, client-side neural network inference.

Zero-Crossing Snapping

Smart boundaries ensure cuts occur at zero voltage, eliminating audible pops and clicks.

3
STAGE 03 • MULTITRACK & DSP EFFECTS
64-Bit Float Engine

Arrange, EQ & Sculpt Dynamic Stems

Organize dialogue, music, and sound design across multitrack lanes. Dial in parametric equalization, compressors, algorithmic reverb, stereo delay, and pitch shifts with real-time FFT visualization.

Multitrack Stems

Independent volume, stereo panning, solo/mute states, and non-destructive clip drag.

Parametric EQ

Multi-band high-pass, low-shelf, peaking, and notch filters with interactive curve manipulation.

Tempo & Loop Snapping

Automated BPM transient detection and musical grid alignment for perfectly seamless loops.

4
STAGE 04 • MASTERING & EXPORT
Universal Formats

Normalize, Encode & Export

Measure loudness against EBU R128 and ITU-R BS.1770 LUFS standards, apply soft limiting to avoid inter-sample peaks, and render to broadcast-grade WAV, 320kbps MP3, or lossless FLAC.

LUFS Loudness Metering

Verify integrated loudness and dynamic range for Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube standards.

Native Encoders

Export directly using LAME MP3, libFLAC, and 32-bit float broadcast WAV engines.

Project Session Files

Save full multitrack sessions as .mixota files with embedded waveforms for later editing.
